How the numbers are built

Job board pages
Every job board has its own page of KPI cards and one tabbed table, built from that board's sheets: LinkedIn_Posting (checked against Linkedin Analysis) and the weekly InMail reports for LinkedIn, Dice_Posting (checked against Dice Analysis) and the daily Dice Candidates sheet for Dice, Indeed Analysis, the Indeed budget and weekly Smart Sourcing for Indeed, week-wise Monster Analysis, Resume-Library Analysis, DocCafe Data and plan usage, Website_Posting, the AlliedTravel Careers report, and weekly Signal Hire, ZoomInfo and weekly Crintell under Other sources. Each extract is checked against its sheet's Grand Total. Dated and weekly rows follow the date filter. Weekly figures (JobDiva resumes, Indeed, AlliedTravel Careers postings, InMails, Monster, Signal Hire, Smart Sourcing, Crintell, Vivian Recruiter Performance) are dated by the Monday their week starts (W40 = Sep 28, W41 = Oct 5), so a week counts in the range or month that contains that Monday. Reports that cover a fixed span, such as ZoomInfo, AlliedTravel Careers and current job slots, do not, and their cards say so. JobDiva Resumes are weekly (W40, W41) and are split into job applications and resume searches; each board's JobDiva tab counts resumes from that source.
Submissions (all pages)
Total submissions come from the SOH sheet in Job Postings.xlsx, by resume source and week. SOH is the full count for a week: it includes submittals of candidates who applied or were found in earlier weeks, not only this week's applicants and searches. Sources ending in "by Applicants" (for example "Dice by Applicants", "LinkedIn Apply Connect by Applicants") count as Job Posting; every other source counts as Resume Search. SOH lists Vivian as a single source, so Vivian submittals all count under Resume Search. SOH is the only source for submittal totals, AlliedTravel Careers included (its own report can show a different count; that figure appears only as "Submissions on Post"). Per-job submission columns from the posting reports (LinkedIn, Dice, Indeed, ATC) are labelled "Submissions on Post", and JobDiva's own submission flag is labelled "Marked Submitted (JobDiva)". Neither is used for totals.
Subscription and ROI
The band at the top of each job board page comes from the SOH sheet in Job Postings.xlsx. The subscription side shows the plan type, monthly cost, views or limits, active post allowance and whether job posting is included. The return-on-investment side uses that sheet's submittals and hires by resume source (weekly). Sources are grouped into boards the same way as JobDiva resumes ("by Applicants" sources count with their board). Cost for the period = monthly cost × 12 ÷ 52 × the number of weeks of SOH data in the selected range, and cost per submittal and per hire divide that cost by the board's submittals and hires. Revenue per hire is not in the files, so ROI is shown as cost per submittal and cost per hire.
Job board colours
Each job board page uses that board's own colour palette (approximate brand colours, darkened where needed so white text stays readable). The Summary page uses the HonorVet brand palette (Brand Red #EE1A0F, Brand Yellow #FDDB26, Brand Gray #808286, Light Gray #D9E0E0, Brand Charcoal #231F20) and shows the HonorVet logo.
Vivian Candidates
The Vivian Candidates card and tab use the Vivian Candidates sheet in Resume Searches.xlsx (one row per candidate, dated by created date): applicants (Inbound and Proposal), submissions, and candidates pushed or not pushed to JobDiva ("Pushed in JobDiva?" = Yes). The sheet is checked against the Vivian Analysis credits report for candidates, credits and submissions.
Vivian Job Board Usage page
Total Active Jobs is the number of rows in the Vivian_Posting sheet (all listings in the file). Total Applicants is the number of candidates in the Vivian Candidates sheet for the selected period, split Inbound / Proposal. This Week Job Posting, the applicants on those postings and the Job Posting tab use Vivian_Posting rows posted in the selected period (Inbound and Proposal columns). Submissions on the Job Posting tab come from the Posting by Recruiters table in Job Postings.xlsx › Vivian Analysis, reported per recruiter for its posting week (Sep 28 – Oct 4). Total Credit Used (Premium | Standard) comes from Resume Searches.xlsx › Vivian Analysis. Pushed to JobDiva comes from the Vivian Candidates sheet. The Manual Proposal Analysis tab uses the manual proposals file (one row per recruiter per Month, updated by hand). It shows the month(s) that overlap the selected range, adding up a recruiter's months and recomputing the response rate; when the range is later than the last month in the file it shows that latest month and says so. The all-recruiters row is the sum of the rows shown.
Total Posts
Jobs in Job Postings.xlsx: the Number of Jobs column of the Vivian Job Posting table, plus one row per job for LinkedIn, Dice, Indeed, Website and DocCafe, dated by posting date.
Total Applicants
Inbound plus manual candidates. Inbound: applications on jobs posted in the period (LinkedIn, Dice, Indeed, Website, DocCafe) plus Vivian Inbound Candidates. Manual: Vivian Proposal Candidates. Vivian total = Inbound + Proposal, which equals Total Candidates in the Posting by Recruiters table.
Resumes Fetched
Candidates in the JobDiva Resumes sheet. Each candidate counts once and is credited to the first recruiter and source that fetched them in the period. Every "HonorVet …" source (quick apply, uploads, general jobs emailed resumes, email merge) counts under "Website"; "Internal" is Internally Sent Resumes (and the Internal source); converted and formatted resumes, iLabor and contact tools sit under "Other". The same mapping applies to submissions from the SOH sheet.
Resumes Searched
Usage reports: LinkedIn InMails sent, Indeed Smart Sourcing and Resume-Library resumes viewed, Dice profiles viewed, Monster fetch credits, Signal Hire unlocks, ZoomInfo contact views, plus Vivian proposals. Units differ by tool. Weekly and monthly reports have no daily detail, so they count whenever the selected range overlaps their report period.
Total Submissions
From postings: submission counts on LinkedIn, Dice and Indeed postings, plus Vivian submissions from the Posting by Recruiters table. From resume searches: JobDiva candidates marked submitted (Vivian rows excluded). Each candidate counts once: a candidate submitted through both paths is counted under postings, and repeat submissions of the same candidate are removed. LinkedIn, Dice and Indeed posting counts carry no candidate names, so they cannot be matched against JobDiva records and some overlap may remain.
Comparison vs previous period
Shown only when the files cover the earlier period. Daily posting and JobDiva data starts on Sep 28, 2026.
Privacy
This page holds counts only. No candidate names, emails or phone numbers are stored in it.
